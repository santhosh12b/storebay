Questions

Before you ask, we've answered.

Do I have to build the store myself?
No — our team builds it for you, start to finish. You just share your product photos, prices, and business details. Once it's live, you get full admin panel access to make changes yourself whenever you want.
Is the .in domain really free?
Yes — included as part of your one-time ₹4,999, registered and connected for you. No separate purchase needed.
What happens after the first free month?
Your subscription continues at ₹999/month from month two onward — no action needed from you, it's automatic.
When does the 0.5% platform fee start?
Only after your first ₹50,000 in lifetime sales. Before that, there's no platform fee at all — and it's never charged on cash-on-delivery orders.
How does the WhatsApp order alert work?
The moment a customer completes an order, you get a WhatsApp message with the order details — no dashboard login needed to know a sale happened.
Can I really download review cards to post on Instagram?
Yes — every review on your store has a download option that generates a clean, branded photo card, ready to post as-is.
