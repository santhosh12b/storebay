Who this is for

Brands that sell, and brands about to.

  • You are launching a brand and want a store that is finished on day one: payments, WhatsApp, COD and shipping already wired in, instead of a free plan whose bill grows as you do.
  • You run a D2C store on Shopify, Wix, Dukaan or WooCommerce, and pay for three or more apps on top of the plan.

Who it is not for

Not everyone, and we would rather say so.

  • A marketplace with many sellers. GoSell is one brand, one store.
  • A business that does not sell physical goods to customers in India. It is built around COD, pincodes, GST invoices and Indian gateways.
  • Somebody who needs a bespoke feature by next week. Custom work is quoted separately and takes its own time.
  • Anyone who wants us to run their ads or write their copy. The tools are in the box; that work is yours.
Why it is sold, not rented

We are paid once. That changes what gets built.

Before you ask

The four questions everyone opens with.

What am I actually paying ₹30,000 for?

Your store, set up and live on our hosting: the full platform, your branding applied, your domain pointed with SSL, every integration built in and waiting for your own keys, the bulk importer for your catalog, and thirty days of support once you are live. The pricing page lists it line by line, including what is not covered.

What do I keep paying after that?

Hosting is included for 1 year, then ₹3,000 a year with us. Beyond that, only what you already pay today and would on any platform: your gateway’s transaction charge, Shiprocket’s rates, WhatsApp’s per-conversation fee — all on your own accounts. There is no licence, no subscription and no share of your revenue, now or later.

How do my products and customers move?

Products through the importer: export a spreadsheet from your current platform, match its columns to ours on screen, and the catalog loads as one file. Customers come across as they place their next order. Order history stays where it is — no platform exports it cleanly, and we would rather say so than promise it.

What if I want to leave?

You already hold the source code and the database. Move them to a server of your own whenever you like, and any PHP developer can carry on from there. Leaving costs nothing, because there is nothing to export — you were never renting.
