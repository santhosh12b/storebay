Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains how refunds work for the one-time store setup fee, the monthly subscription, and the platform fee.

1. One-Time Setup Fee (₹4,999)

If work has not yet started
You'll receive a full refund. If a domain has already been purchased on your behalf, the domain cost will be deducted from your refund, and the domain will be handed over to you to manage going forward.
If your store has already been delivered
The ₹4,999 setup fee is non-refundable once your store has been fully built and delivered.

2. Monthly Subscription (₹999 + GST)

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your store will remain live until the end of the period you've already paid for (for example, if you've paid through 30th September, your store stays active until then). Once that period ends, your store will be paused and taken offline until you resubscribe.

3. Platform Fee (0.5% + GST)

The platform fee, applicable only after your first ₹50,000 in lifetime sales, is not refundable once charged, as it applies to completed, successful payments.

4. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 5–7 business days to your original payment method.

5. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Refund Policy at any time. Continued use of our services after changes are posted means you accept the updated terms.
