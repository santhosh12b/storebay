Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Storebay ("we", "us", "our") is a trade name operated by Narumugai Foods (GSTIN: 33AAUFN8937H1ZM). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from sellers using our services, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. What We Collect

When you sign up or work with our team to build your store, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, and email address
  • Your business name and business details
  • PAN and other documents required to set up your payment gateway, shipping, or GST invoicing
  • Your WhatsApp Business number
  • Product photos, descriptions, and pricing you share with us to build your store

2. How Information Is Shared With Us

To keep things simple, we collect your details and product content through WhatsApp, Google Drive, or email — whichever is easiest for you.

Please do not share sensitive information such as passwords, OTPs, or full card details with us over any channel. Always verify who you're speaking with before sharing anything sensitive.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to build and set up your online store — including connecting your payment gateway, courier partner, and WhatsApp order notifications. Your details are accessible only to our internal team.

4. Payments

We do not collect or store your customers' payment information. All payments are processed directly through your own Razorpay or Cashfree account. Storebay never has access to card numbers or sensitive payment data.

5. Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand how our website is used, and may add other analytics tools in the future as needed.

6. Third Parties We Work With

Depending on your store's setup, your information may be shared with: Razorpay, Cashfree, Nimbuspost, Shiprocket, GoDaddy, and Hostinger — the partners we use to power payments, shipping, domains, and hosting.

7. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is hosted in India. We retain your information for as long as you remain active on our platform, and for 2 years afterward, after which it is deleted.

8. Your Rights

You can reach out to us anytime to ask what information we hold about you, or to request corrections. Contact us at info@theeditingcrew.in.

9. Age Requirement

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We recommend checking this page periodically for any changes.
